Long-shot fades by category

Same rules in every category: the bot takes every market that qualifies, so the counts differ only because Kalshi lists more of some kinds. What makes the comparison fair is enough independent events per category. Events are distinct settlement days or games; several strikes on the same day count once. Enough data = 300+ events, Getting there = 100+, Too early = under 100. The bot keeps paper-testing every category automatically; Enough data by is the date each one should reach 300 at its pace over the last two weeks.
